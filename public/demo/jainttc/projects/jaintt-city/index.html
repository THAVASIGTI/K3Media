Discover your dream land

Own a plot. Build your future. Live with confidence.

Welcome to Jaintt City, a thoughtfully planned DTCP-approved residential layout located in Alampatti. Designed for families and smart investors, this premium plotted development offers well-laid roads, essential infrastructure, and the flexibility to either build your dream home or choose our affordable 2BHK villa model. With plot sizes starting from 600 sq.ft and villa options starting at just ₹20 Lakhs, Jaintt City makes homeownership accessible without compromising on quality.

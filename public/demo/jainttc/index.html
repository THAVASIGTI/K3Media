From plot to home

Scroll slowly: a Jaintt villa goes up the way a real site does, steel, brick and slab, from the approved plot to the keys.

  1. Stage 1 of 7

    An approved plot

    Pegs and string mark a DTCP & RERA approved plot with clear title. Bricks, sand and cement arrive on site.

  2. Stage 2 of 7

    Footings and steel

    Concrete footings go in under every column, then steel rebar cages are tied and stood upright, and the plinth is poured.

  3. Stage 3 of 7

    Brick by brick

    Columns are cast round the steel, then the walls rise course by course in running bond, with scaffolding alongside.

  4. Stage 4 of 7

    Shuttering and slab

    Plywood shuttering on steel props, a mesh of rebar laid across it, then the first-floor slab is poured.

  5. Stage 5 of 7

    Upper floor and roof

    Steel and columns again, the upper walls, then roof shuttering and the roof slab, the same care at every level.

  6. Stage 6 of 7

    Plaster and paint

    Shuttering is struck, plaster and paint cover the brickwork, the scaffolding comes down and the site is cleared.

  7. Stage 7 of 7

    Your dream home

    Glazing, timber, gates and garden: the corner villa you see here stands in Thirumangalam today.

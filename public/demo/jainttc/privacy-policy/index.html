Privacy policy

How Jaintt Constructions collects, uses and protects your information. Last updated July 28, 2026.

1. Introduction

Jaintt Constructions (“Company”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you visit jainttconstructions.in or communicate with us through our website, WhatsApp, Meta Lead Ads, or other digital channels.

2. Information we collect

Personal information. We may collect:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Address (if voluntarily provided)
  • Any information you submit through enquiry or contact forms.

Non-personal information. We may automatically collect:

  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Website usage statistics
  • Cookies and analytics data

3. How we use your information

Your information may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiries.
  • Provide information about our projects and services.
  • Schedule site visits or customer consultations.
  • Communicate through phone, email, WhatsApp, or SMS.
  • Improve our website and customer experience.
  • Send project updates, promotional offers, or important announcements where permitted by law.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share your information only with:

  • Authorized employees handling customer enquiries.
  • Trusted technology providers such as WhatsApp Business API, CRM platforms, hosting providers, and customer support systems.
  • Government authorities where required under applicable law.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve functionality, enhance user experience, and analyze website traffic.

You may disable cookies through your browser settings; however, some features of the website may not function properly.

6. Data security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information.

Although we strive to secure your data, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to legal or business obligations.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications.

8. Third-party websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.

9. Changes to this privacy policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be published on this page with the revised “Last Updated” date.

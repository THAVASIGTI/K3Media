Five stages of a Jaintt home

What happens at each stage, and what is in place before the next one starts.

  1. Stage 1 of 5

    An approved plot

    Every Jaintt home starts on a DTCP & RERA approved layout with a clear title, so the land is ready to register and to build on.

    • Clear title & legal documentation
    • Plots from 1 to 5 cents
    • Blacktop roads, solar lights and drainage in place
    • Ready for immediate registration

  2. Stage 2 of 5

    Design and drawings

    Our architects draw a home that balances Madurai’s traditions with modern living: Vaastu compliant, open to light and air, east or north facing.

    • Vaastu-compliant floor plans
    • East and north facing options
    • Eco-friendly design choices
    • Drawings reviewed with you

  3. Stage 3 of 5

    Foundation and frame

    Skilled craftsmen build to the drawings with premium materials and proven techniques, from plinth to roof slab.

    • Proven construction techniques
    • Premium materials
    • Work checked at every stage
    • Timely delivery

  4. Stage 4 of 5

    Finish and interiors

    Joinery, wardrobes, cladding, glazing and colour, chosen with you and finished by our artisans.

    • Personalised interiors
    • Timber cladding and joinery
    • Premium finishes
    • Built to suit how you live

  5. Stage 5 of 5

    Handover

    Gates hung, garden planted, keys handed over: a home in a gated community with security, parking and space for children.

    • Gated community with 24x7 security
    • CCTV surveillance
    • Garden retreat and kids’ zone
    • Easy parking

The build, in photographs

  1. The plot

  2. The plan

    Vaastu-compliant drawings, east or north facing, made around your family.

    Steel, shuttering and a roof slab poured by our own site team.

  4. The frame

    Columns and walls rise, checked at every stage against the drawings.

  5. The finish

    Joinery, wardrobes, tiles and paint, chosen with you.

    A finished home on a gated street, gates hung and garden planted.

    Families settle in, and the layout becomes a street.

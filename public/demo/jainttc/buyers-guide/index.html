What DTCP and RERA approval mean

DTCP approval

Directorate of Town and Country Planning, Tamil Nadu

DTCP approves residential layouts outside the Chennai metropolitan area. An approved layout has been checked for road widths, open space, drainage and plot sizes, and carries an approval number on the layout plan.

What to check

  • Ask for the layout approval number and the approved layout plan.
  • Match your plot number and size against the approved plan.
  • An approved layout makes bank loans and building permission far simpler.

RERA registration

Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 · TNRERA

RERA protects buyers of registered projects: the developer must register the project, disclose its approvals and timelines, and keep to what is registered. The registration number should appear on the project's brochure and advertising.

What to check

  • Note the project's RERA registration number.
  • Look the project up on the Tamil Nadu RERA portal.
  • Keep copies of the agreement and every payment receipt.

Jaintt City and Jaintt Enclave are both DTCP and RERA approved layouts.

How a home loan works

  1. 1

    Check eligibility

    Your bank looks at income, existing loans and age to set how much it can lend and for how long.

  2. 2

    Choose the property

    Approved plots and homes in approved layouts are what banks prefer to lend on.

  3. 3

    Legal and technical check

    The bank's lawyer reviews the documents and its engineer values the property.

  4. 4

    Sanction

    The bank issues a sanction letter with the amount, rate and tenure.

  5. 5

    Registration and disbursement

    You pay your share, the sale is registered, and the bank releases the loan.

Loan amounts, rates and fees differ between banks and change over time; your bank will confirm them in writing.
